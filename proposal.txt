Project Overview: My project creates a simulation of a flower shop. The goal of this program is to help the flower shop better understand their inventory and supplier needs.
Implementation: I will use a std::map to store and mange data related to a particular flower shop. The std::lists will keep track of customers who come in and what they order, flowers currently in the flower shop inventory, and flowers growing in the supplier's greenhouse.
Simulated events: The simulation will focus on three events: A customer arrives and orders a flower arrangement, flowers in the supplier's greenhouse are ready to go and are added to the inventory, and the shop gives a customer in the queue their order.
